## The rules

- Every must belongs to your core flow. If it doesn't, it's a should.
- If a must slips past its phase, move a should out, not sleep.
- New idea mid-sprint? It goes under won't first. Move it up only if something else comes down.
- A must is done when it works on your live link, on a phone, not on your laptop.
