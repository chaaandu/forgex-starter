# Your research doc

**Copy this into a Google Doc, go through it with your mentor, then add the link in the ForgeX portal.**

You research before you build anything. This template is the shape of that research: who you talked to, what they said, and what you saw. Every `[bracket]` is yours to fill in.

## How to use it

1. Go to [docs.google.com](https://docs.google.com) and start a blank doc. Name it `[Your name] · Kirana research`.
2. Copy everything below the line into it, from **The challenge** down.
3. Fill in every `[bracket]`. Replace the brackets too.
4. Click **Share**, and set it so anyone with the link can comment.
5. Go through it with your mentor.
6. Add the link in the ForgeX portal.

**How to talk to people:**

- Ask about the last time it happened, not what usually happens.
- Write down their words, not your summary of them.
- Don't pitch an idea. You're here to listen. Your idea comes after, in [`02-your-solution.md`](02-your-solution.md).
- Ask before you write down a name. A first name and an area is enough.

## The challenge

> **2 lakh kiranas closed in a single year.** A retailer federation says 2 lakh kiranas closed in a year, and 60% of Mumbai grocery stores near quick-commerce dark stores saw volumes fall. Buying through distributors and stocking only 1,000–1,500 items, a kirana cannot match dark-store prices or range. It needs hundreds of loyal households to survive on thin margins, so a handful of defections can be enough to close it.
>
> **The challenge:** Help a kirana keep its loyal households ordering from it rather than from the nearest dark store.
