# Your flows

A **flow** is what one person does in your app, from the moment something makes them open it to the moment they're done. Each step becomes a screen, or part of one.

Every `[bracket]` is yours to fill in.

## How to pick the one core flow

1. **List every flow you can think of.** One line each: who, trigger, end state. Don't fill in steps yet.
2. **Ask 3 questions of each:**
   - Does it happen at the moment your research found, in section 4 of your research doc?
   - Would your person do it every week without being asked?
   - Could you build it, end to end, by 23 Oct?
3. **The core flow is the one with 3 yeses.** If two tie, pick the one your owner talked about most.
4. **Write the others under What you will NOT build** in [`02-your-solution.md`](02-your-solution.md). They aren't gone. They're later.

**Building 2 or 3 connected flows?** Fill in one template for each. Under **End state**, say which flow it hands on to, and what it hands on.

## Check it

- Can you say the whole flow out loud in under 30 seconds?
- Does every step lead to the next one without a gap?
- Is the end state something your owner would notice?
