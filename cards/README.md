## How the cards fit together

- **Cards 1–9 need no database.** Until card 10, the app runs on sample data from `lib/sample.ts`, and a dark note at the top of every screen says so. Everything you build in those days works and shows on your live link.
- **Card 10 connects Supabase.** The app switches from sample data to the database on its own, once the keys are set. Nothing you built is lost.
- **Card 11 moves your own screen's data across**, if your card 5 screen added sample data of its own.

## How to use a card

- Each card has the same parts: the task, why it matters, a prompt, what you'll see when it works, the 2 most likely errors, and a commit message.
- In a prompt, words in **[square brackets]** are yours to fill in. Replace them, brackets and all.
- Paste prompts into the chat in **Cursor**. They work in **Copilot Chat** too.
- Use **Claude** to think and plan: what to build, what an error means, whether a number adds up.
- If something breaks that the card doesn't cover, copy the whole error into Cursor or Claude and ask what it means before you change anything.
- Commit and push at the end of every card. Your repo should show work on every day.
