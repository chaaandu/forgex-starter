# Write my README

**When to use it:** on card 14, once a real owner has used your app.

Replace every `[bracket]`, brackets and all. Paste it into the chat in Cursor.

```text
Write a first draft of my README case study.

Read:
- frameworks/07-case-study.md (the template; follow its sections exactly)
- frameworks/02-your-solution.md and frameworks/03-flows.md
- the README.md that's there now

From my research doc:
[paste the parts you want to use: the owners, the regulars, the moment,
and the quotes]

From my owner's week with the app:
[what they did with it, the numbers, what they said, what broke]

My live link: [ ] · My Loom link: [ ]

1. Move the starter's setup steps out of README.md into a new file, SETUP.md,
   word for word.
2. Write README.md from the template. Fill every bracket from what I gave
   you. Where I gave you nothing, leave the [bracket] for me. Never make up
   a number, a quote or a result.
3. Write it in the first person, as me. Short sentences, plain words,
   British spelling, no exclamation marks.
Then list the brackets I still have to fill in.
```

**When it works:** your README reads as the story of your app, and every number and quote in it is real.
